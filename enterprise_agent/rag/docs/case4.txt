Client: Logistics Company

Industry: Supply Chain

Problem:
Shipment tracking systems were not connected with warehouse management systems.
Lack of visibility in real-time tracking.

Tech Stack:
IoT Devices, Warehouse Software, GPS Systems

Solution:
Integrated IoT data with cloud-based analytics platform.
Used Kafka for real-time streaming.
Built centralized dashboard.

Outcome:
Real-time shipment tracking.
Improved operational efficiency.
Better decision-making with analytics.
